Treatment menu

Comprehensive dental care, elevated by precision.

Every service includes careful diagnostics, transparent planning, and comfort-first delivery.

◌

Teeth Cleaning

Professional hygiene therapy removes plaque, stain, and calculus while refreshing gum health and lowering long-term disease risk.

Benefits

  • Brighter enamel and fresher breath
  • Early gum disease detection
  • Lower cavity and inflammation risk

Treatment process

Oral exam, ultrasonic scaling, polishing, flossing, fluoride option, and a personalized home-care plan.

Pricing preview: from ₹2,500
✦

Cosmetic Dentistry

Smile design, veneers, bonding, and contouring crafted to improve shape, shade, balance, and confidence without over-treatment.

Benefits

  • Natural-looking facial harmony
  • Conservative enamel-preserving options
  • Preview-led treatment planning

Treatment process

Consultation, photographs, digital smile design, mock-up preview, final preparation, and bonding or restoration placement.

Pricing preview: from ₹7,500
◆

Dental Implants

Digitally planned implants replace missing teeth with stable, natural-feeling restorations designed for function and aesthetics.

Benefits

  • Protects jawbone volume
  • Restores confident chewing
  • Long-lasting fixed solution

Treatment process

CBCT scan, guided surgical planning, implant placement, healing review, custom abutment, and crown delivery.

Pricing preview: from ₹42,000
◇

Orthodontics

Clear aligners and modern braces improve bite alignment, smile symmetry, and long-term oral stability.

Benefits

  • Discreet tooth movement
  • Improved bite comfort
  • Digitally monitored progress

Treatment process

Scan, bite analysis, simulation, aligner delivery, progress checks, refinements, and retention planning.

Pricing preview: from ₹65,000
✧

Root Canal Treatment

Comfort-focused endodontic care relieves infection and preserves natural teeth using modern rotary systems and digital imaging.

Benefits

  • Relieves pain and infection
  • Preserves natural tooth structure
  • Prevents extraction where possible

Treatment process

Diagnosis, local anesthesia, canal cleaning, shaping, sealing, and crown recommendation when needed.

Pricing preview: from ₹8,500
☼

Teeth Whitening

Clinician-supervised whitening lifts stains safely while protecting sensitivity and maintaining natural enamel character.

Benefits

  • Visible brightness in one visit
  • Custom sensitivity management
  • Maintenance guidance included

Treatment process

Shade assessment, gum protection, whitening application, activation cycles, final shade review, and aftercare kit.

Pricing preview: from ₹12,000
⬡

Dental Crowns

Premium ceramic crowns restore strength, function, and aesthetics for damaged, root-treated, or heavily restored teeth.

Benefits

  • Protects weakened teeth
  • Custom shade and contour
  • Durable ceramic materials

Treatment process

Preparation, digital impression, provisional crown, laboratory fabrication, final fit, and bite refinement.

Pricing preview: from ₹15,000
+

Emergency Dental Care

Same-day urgent dental support for pain, swelling, broken teeth, trauma, lost crowns, and sudden infection symptoms.

Benefits

  • Rapid pain relief
  • Stabilizes urgent conditions
  • Clear next-step treatment plan

Treatment process

Triage, focused examination, imaging if required, immediate stabilization, medication where appropriate, and follow-up booking.

Pricing preview: from ₹3,500
